# WeChat Official Account Headline Growth Rank / gzh-toutiao-growth-rank

---

## Introduction

The WeChat Official Account Headline Growth Rank (formerly the Black Horse Rank) is a growth radar for official accounts: it counts headline reading growth week over week (this week vs. last week) and ranks accounts in descending order of headline growth rate, helping you quickly spot black-horse accounts that are surging this week.

**Key Value**

- 📈 Week-over-week comparison: this week's headline reads vs. last week's — growth at a glance
- 🐎 Black-horse discovery: ranked by headline growth rate in descending order, the fastest-growing accounts come first
- 🔖 Trend labels: each account's rank change vs. the previous period (up / down / unchanged / new entry)
- 🗂️ Category filtering: 23 author categories, with a combined all-category rank or a single-category rank

**Who It's For**

- 🧑‍💻 Content operators — quickly lock onto growth black horses in your niche and follow up
- 📣 Brands / MCNs — discover fast-growing accounts for promotion and partnership decisions
- ✍️ Official account creators — learn from the content direction of high-growth accounts

---

## Features

### Core Features

- 📊 **Weekly headline growth rank**: counts headline reading growth by natural week (Monday–Sunday)
- 🏆 **Growth-rate descending ranking**: fixed descending order by headline growth rate, fastest accounts first
- 🗂️ **23 author categories**: humanities, health & wellness, tech and more; combined all-category rank by default
- 📄 **Page-by-page browsing**: 20 accounts per page, up to 5 pages
- 🔖 **Trend labels**: each account's rank change vs. the previous period (up / down / unchanged / new entry)
- 💬 **In-chat ranking output**: the ranking is fully displayed as a table right in the conversation

---

## How to Use

Describe what you want in natural language — no commands to memorize.

### Quick Phrases

| Intent | Example | Result |
| ------ | ------- | ------ |
| View the latest rank | "Show me the official account headline growth rank" | Outputs the latest combined all-category rank |
| Filter by category | "Health & wellness headline growth rank" | Outputs only that category's rank |
| Specify a period | "The week of Sep 7" | Queries that natural week (auto-normalized to Monday) |
| Next page | "Next page" | Outputs the next 20 accounts |
| List categories | "What categories are there?" | Lists all 23 author categories |

## Important Data Notes

- The ranking updates every natural week; the period runs from Monday to Sunday
- Each page has a fixed 20 accounts, up to 5 pages
- Usually only the latest period is kept; trend labels only appear when two adjacent periods both have data
